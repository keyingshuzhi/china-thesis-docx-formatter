# Chinese Thesis Formatting

The official school or journal template overrides every generic convention.
Chinese theses often use a cover, Chinese and English abstracts, a table of
contents, chapter headings, figures and tables, references, acknowledgements,
and appendices, but their required formatting varies materially by institution.

For domestic degree theses, first identify the degree level. Undergraduate
papers commonly include bilingual abstracts/keywords, contents, references,
acknowledgements, and appendices. Master's and doctoral theses often also show
an originality/authorization page and a page for research achievements during
degree study. These are review prompts, not universal rules: a school's template
and written guide decide presence, naming, order, pagination, and formatting.

When the template does not demonstrate a component, keep its rule absent and
add an extraction warning. Ask the user for the written guide or confirmation
rather than guessing.

The generated JSON records observed styles and role mappings. It is intentionally
portable: it contains no school name and can be kept with the thesis project as
the auditable formatting profile.
